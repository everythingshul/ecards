Why It Works

Everything tracked, nothing lost

A single system that keeps contracts, approvals, cards, and spend all in one place.

E-Signed Agreements

Shuls, applicants, and stores can all sign their agreements electronically, with a legally attributable signature, timestamp, and IP address.

Live Card & Spend Tracking

Card balances and store-level spend sync automatically, so everyone always sees an up-to-date picture.

Fast Approvals

Admins review, approve, and allocate card amounts in a few clicks, with automatic email notifications at every step.

Role-Based Access

Shuls, stores, and staff each see only what's relevant to them, with field-level permissions for sensitive data.

Full Data Exports

Every list can be downloaded in full, plus one complete system export covering every table, whenever you need it.

Tasks & Reminders

Assign follow-ups to your team with due dates and automatic reminder emails, so nothing falls through the cracks.
